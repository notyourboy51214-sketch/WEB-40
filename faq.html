Client FAQ

Answers to operational and business inquiries.

Our headquarters is in Rawalpindi, but we possess the logistics and workforce to execute projects across major urban centers in Pakistan.

Do you provide warranties on your work?

Absolutely. Every contract includes a standard SLA and warranty period ensuring any defects or operational issues are resolved immediately at no extra cost.

How do we initiate a project?

Contact us via WhatsApp or submit a formal Request for Proposal (RFP) through our Contact page. Our estimating team will return a commercial bid within 48 hours.
